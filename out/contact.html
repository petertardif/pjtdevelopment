Before you ask

Common questions.

Do I need to know what I want built? +

Not at all. Most clients come with a problem, not a spec — 'my team wastes hours on X.' Figuring out the right solution is my job, and that's exactly what the discovery call is for.

How much does a project cost? +

It depends on what you need, but you'll always get a fixed, written quote before any work starts — no surprises. After our call I can give you a realistic ballpark quickly.

How long does it take? +

Small tools and automations can take a couple of weeks; larger apps take longer. I'll give you an honest timeline as part of the written plan, and you'll see working demos along the way.

Will I be able to understand what's going on? +

Yes — that's the whole point of PJT. I came from teaching, so I explain everything in plain English and check that it actually makes sense to you. Ask 'why?' as often as you like.

What happens after launch? +

I don't disappear. I'm available for fixes, tweaks, and new features as your business grows — and the same person who built it is the one who supports it.
